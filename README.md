# The World Dungeon

A personal couch co-op brawler inspired by *Dungeon Crawler Carl* by Matt Dinniman. Made for fun, not for sale or distribution.

**Play:** https://tgstreet.github.io/world-dungeon/

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new icon and turn the phone sideways.

## Controllers
Pair Xbox, PlayStation or MFi controllers to the phone in Settings > Bluetooth.
X attack · A jump · B/Y special · Start pause. Up to 4 players.

## Play on Apple TV
Pair controllers to the iPhone, open the game, then use Screen Mirroring from Control Center to AirPlay it to the TV.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole game
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icons/` – make it installable and playable offline
